Why a graph database?

Skills, mentors and projects form a dense web of connections. Questions like “who can help on this project given overlapping skills?” or “what is the shortest learning path from TypeScript to Kubernetes?” are natural multi-hop traversals in a graph — and awkward recursive joins in SQL.
